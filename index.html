MODEL CARD

What the engine actually does

Preference learningPairwise choices update latent criterion/option utilities using a logistic Bradley–Terry-style update with uncertainty shrinkage.
UncertaintyEach score is a triangular distribution (low / likely / high). Monte Carlo samples propagate score, weight and risk uncertainty.
Active learningQuestions are selected using ambiguity, posterior uncertainty and expected ranking impact; low-value questions are skipped.
MemoryRelated criteria are linked with local feature-hashed vectors. No server-side model is required.
RobustnessRisk, regret, Pareto efficiency, sensitivity and scenario analysis expose where assumptions drive the result.
Learning loopOutcome feedback updates calibration, prediction error categories, long-term preferences and decision freshness.
