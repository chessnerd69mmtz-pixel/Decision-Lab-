MODEL CARD

How Decision Lab learns

1. Pairwise learningChoices are converted into preference observations and updated with a Bradley–Terry-style logistic model.
2. Active learningThe engine prefers questions that are ambiguous and likely to change the ranking, reducing unnecessary questions.
3. Semantic memoryFeature-hashed local text vectors connect related criteria across decisions without uploading your text.
4. Consequence learningOutcome satisfaction becomes a reward signal that gradually calibrates your long-term preference profile.
5. RobustnessMonte Carlo sampling and sensitivity analysis reveal where the result is stable and where assumptions matter.
